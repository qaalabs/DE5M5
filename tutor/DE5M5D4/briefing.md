## EPA Presentation Briefing

**Docs: day4/presentation.md**

- They pick the KSB(s), not you - one or a small combination
- Their own work is the evidence - explain *how* it evidences the KSB, not just *what* it does
- At least 5 minutes each, maximum 10 - tell them the time per person once you know the headcount
- Rule of thumb: 100 minutes of presenting time divided by headcount, capped at 10 (18 learners = 5 min, 12 = 8 min, 10 or fewer = 10 min)
- 40 minutes to prepare, until lunch
- Confirm the running order informally at the start of lunch
