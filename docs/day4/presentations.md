# Afternoon Presentations

This afternoon you'll present your library pipeline as evidence against the apprenticeship standard.

## Step 1: Pick your KSB(s)

Read the [ST1386 Data Engineer standard](https://skillsengland.education.gov.uk/apprenticeship-standards/st1386-v1-0) and choose **one Knowledge, Skill, or Behaviour - or a small combination of them** - that your work over the last four days evidences well.

There's no fixed list - pick whatever your pipeline actually demonstrates. Some options that tend to fit this project well:

- Data ingestion and pipeline design
- Testing and code quality
- CI/CD and deployment
- Problem solving and troubleshooting
- Continuous improvement

## Step 2: Prepare (until lunch)

You have about 40 minutes. For your chosen KSB(s):

- Show the part of your pipeline that's the evidence
- Be ready to explain *how* it evidences the KSB, not just *what* it does

## Step 3: Present

- Presentations run in groups of 3, with a short break between groups
- Aim for around 10 minutes each - some will run faster, some slower, that's fine
- As you present, the trainer will share the formal KSB text in chat so everyone can follow along
