# Afternoon Presentations

This afternoon you'll present using the EPA discussion topics as a guide.

## Step 1: Pick your KSB(s)

Read the [ST1386 Data Engineer standard](https://skillsengland.education.gov.uk/apprenticeship-standards/st1386-v1-0) and choose **one Knowledge, Skill, or Behaviour - or a small combination of them** - that your work over the last four days evidences well.

There's no fixed list - pick whatever your feel comfortable with. Some options that tend to fit this module well:

- Data ingestion and pipeline design
- Testing and code quality
- CI/CD and deployment
- Problem solving and troubleshooting
- Continuous improvement

## Step 2: Prepare (until lunch)

You have about 40 minutes to prepare.

For your chosen KSB(s):

- Be ready to explain *how* it evidences the KSB, not just *what* it does.
- Show how it relates to this week if possible, or to your workplace if you can.

## Step 3: Present

- Presentations run in groups of 4, with a short break between groups
- At least 5 minutes each, maximum of 10 - your trainer will confirm the time per person before lunch
